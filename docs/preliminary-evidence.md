# Preliminary evidence and limits

These are development observations from the pre-release experimental harness, not results generated by the synthetic smoke test. They motivate feasibility and the proposed study; they do not establish SciReM superiority. Raw source datasets, account transcripts and private run directories are not distributed here. Full empirical independent reproduction remains contingent on a documented accessible dataset and complete benchmark manifests.

| Check | Observed result | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Actual next-analysis execution | Seven pooled conditional-logit flag/complement interactions; all49 aggregate outputs reproduced by independently written package-based R | Requested interaction analyses were actually executed with specified strata/clustered uncertainty | Causal identification, original-paper rediscovery, or seven independent discoveries |
| Fixed-result evidence presentation | One case; three fresh calls in each of four conditions. Evidence acknowledgement: original0/3; table3/3; instruction1/3; both3/3 | This evidence-use failure was responsive to presentation | General memory/PPR effect, overall quality improvement, or TSV alone (salience/redundancy/recency also changed) |
| Full-loop follow-up | Both arms encountered engineering failures; table arm omitted a failed extra diagnostic while interpreting a successful primary result | Completed primary analysis and requested diagnostic completion must be distinguished | Reduced repetition or a reliable efficiency advantage |
| Real error-to-repair replay | Two actual failed diagnostics repaired in one model call each;11/11 outputs matched numeric-preservation controls;349 connected tests passed | Source-grounded error/code handback can recover these failures | A subsequent entire loop was fixed, or complete scientific adequacy of every requested contrast |

The fixed-result study used Luna medium,12 isolated Decision calls, the same supplied evidence, and condition-concealed ratings by the developer, not independent human evaluators. Its initial table intervention appended29 already supplied metrics. The seven-analysis execution used one real model call to produce R and22.13seconds overall; subsequent independent validation was separate. Samples differed across flags because missingness differed. Pooled contrasts restrict non-interacted slopes to be equal across groups.

The last full-loop version had four completed Decisions in the original-presentation arm and one in the table arm before observed error-triggered stops. Original-presentation reasoning developed from one subgroup interaction to a time-flag interaction to a joint test, recognizing a sign contradiction. These are meaningful adaptations even without the added table. Unequal error-stopped trajectories and n=1 per arm do not support a general performance ranking. Earlier versions and failed QA were retained, not silently replaced by repaired successes.

The final repair replay preserved each submitted specification and numeric output; one coefficient-change diagnostic still lacked uncertainty for the cross-model difference. Therefore “code ran”, “numbers reproduced”, “requested test fully fulfilled” and “scientifically defensible conclusion” remain separate outcomes.

No human-review efficiency, novelty gain, general token saving, or reliable original-paper rediscovery advantage has yet been established. Use this table as feasibility evidence, with explicitly prospective evaluation in the proposal.
